# ShopFlow Design Specification

## Overview
ShopFlow uses a warm, high-contrast, editorial DTC retail aesthetic. Products are the hero content, framed by clean hairline borders, generous spacing, and crisp dark forest & purple accent highlights.

## Colors
- **Canvas (`#FAFAF7`)**: Warm off-white page background.
- **Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean white card & modal surfaces.
- **Ink (`#14140F`)**: Deep near-black primary text for high contrast.
- **Brand Green (`#1F4D3A`)**: Forest green primary CTA buttons & primary badges.
- **Accent Purple (`#7C3AED`)**: Secondary brand highlight used in badges and active tabs.

## Typography
- Font Family: **Inter** (system fallback: `-apple-system`, `sans-serif`).
- High contrast, tight tracking on headings, accessible line height on body.

## Components
- **Buttons**: Rounded-xl (12px), bold font, hover transform micro-animations.
- **Cards**: Surface background (`#FFFFFF`), hairline border (`#E2E8F0`), subtle shadow on hover.
